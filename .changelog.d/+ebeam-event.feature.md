**New EVENT-ONLY flavour `cspdk.si220_ebeam_event` for the CORNERSTONE e-beam workshop (#424).** It isn't for regular MPW submissions, and its rules are provisional. SOI 220 with a single 120 nm e-beam etch: cores on 3/0, grating teeth on 6/0, exposure on 4/0. It has cells (straight, bends, coupler, ring, the student-batch grating and a period/fill-factor sweep, and a 6 × 3 mm die with placeholder I/O), SAX models (rib n_g 3.85, 2.5 dB/cm), `post_process()` for the foundry's 4/0 merge, and `check()` for the event rules: 200 nm feature and gap on 3/0, 4/0 and 6/0, at most 2,000,000 µm² of post-processed exposure, and the 6 × 3 mm design area. `python -m cspdk.si220_ebeam_event.tapeout` checks GDS files, reports the exposure budget and writes the post-processed layouts.
